The problem

Forecasts say where the storm goes.
Responders need to know what breaks.

Fani 2019

Puri's power-distribution network largely destroyed; prolonged outages in Puri, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack.

Hudhud 2014

Visakhapatnam's power and telecom networks collapsed for days; ~₹21,900 crore estimated losses.

Amphan 2020

Sundarbans and Kolkata hit by surge, wind and tree-fall — widespread power and telecom failure.

  • Warnings are issued per district; decisions are made per asset — this hospital, that substation.
  • Failures cascade: an intact hospital goes dark when its feeder substation trips.
  • Pre-positioning generators, crews and shelters needs a ranked list 24–72 h ahead, with honest uncertainty.

The solution

Track forecast → asset-level risk → action

  • Hazard at every asset: wind, storm surge, rainfall flood
  • 40-member ensemble — probabilities, not guesses
  • Fragility curves for 9 infrastructure classes
  • Cascade through the power-dependency network
  • Ranked actions: evacuate, protect, restore, connect
  • Runs in the browser in < 1 s · zero install

How it works

A physics-based impact chain

1 · Track & ensemble

IMD track, hourly. Monte-Carlo members with error σ ≈ 45 km at 24 h, ±12 % intensity. Kaplan–DeMaria decay over land.

→

2 · Hazards

Holland (1980) wind + forward-motion asymmetry + terrain. Shelf-amplified surge. R-CLIPER-style rain → flood depth.

→

3 · Fragility

Lognormal wind & flood curves per asset class (HAZUS-MH derived) → P(damage).

→

4 · Cascade

Substation ← transmission line; hospitals, water, telecom ← substation, net of backup → P(disruption).

→

5 · Decisions

KPIs + actions ranked by risk × criticality × people served.

Key insight

Infrastructure fails as a network

→

→
Hospital12 % damage → 36 % disruption
  • The building survives — the service doesn't.
  • Backup power is modelled explicitly: hospital DG sets 60 %, telecom batteries 50 %, water 30 %.
  • Output is an action, not a map: "Pre-position DG sets + 72 h fuel at 2 hospitals in Puri — grid feeder likely to trip."

Who uses it

Built for the 72 hours before landfall

SDMAs & district collectors

Where to evacuate first, which shelters are themselves exposed, how many people to move.

Power utilities (discoms)

Which substations and line spans will fail → stage crews and mobile transformers in advance.

Hospitals & health dept.

Which hospitals lose grid power → fuel, generators, patient transfers.

Telecom operators

Towers likely to fail → cell-on-wheels and satellite phones for first responders.

Ports & industry

Surge and wind exposure of terminals → suspend operations, secure vessels.

Planners & insurers

What-if storms to stress-test the coast and prioritise retrofits.

Roadmap & tech

From prototype to production

  • Live IMD / JTWC advisories and ECMWF / NCMRWF ensembles
  • Real assets from OpenStreetMap, Bhuvan and discom GIS (prototype inventory is synthetic)
  • DEM + coastline for surge and inundation
  • Fragility calibration on Fani, Amphan, Hudhud, Biparjoy, Michaung losses
  • SMS / WhatsApp alerts to control rooms

Stack

  • Vanilla JavaScript model — no server, works offline
  • Leaflet map · GitHub Pages hosting
  • Node test runner — physics + hindcast tests
  • Playwright — automated demo recording
