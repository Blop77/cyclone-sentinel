The problem

Warnings say where the storm goes.
Responders need to know what breaks, before it does.

Fani 2019

Puri's power-distribution network largely destroyed; prolonged outages in Puri, Bhubaneswar and Cuttack.

Hudhud 2014

Visakhapatnam's power and telecom networks collapsed for days; about ₹21,900 crore in estimated losses.

Amphan 2020

Surge, wind and tree-fall across the Sundarbans and Kolkata; widespread grid and road failure.

  • Warnings are issued per district; decisions are made per asset: this hospital, that substation, this road.
  • Failures cascade: rain floods an arterial road → a standing hospital is cut off; a feeder trips → it goes dark.
  • Shifting from post-landfall recovery to pre-landfall action needs asset-level risk 24–72 h ahead, and a way to tell the right authority.
CycloneSentinel2

The solution

One platform, four jobs

  • Simulate wind, storm surge and rainfall flooding at 511 assets with a 40-member ensemble
  • Map exposure of power grids, arterial roads, hospitals and cyclone shelters
  • Trace rainfall damage pathways: rain → road flooded → facility cut off
  • Reason with Gemini over satellite imagery + model output
  • Auto-dispatch multilingual advisories to district, municipal and state authorities
Fani (2019) replay: real NASA VIIRS imagery under the forecast wind swath3

How it works

Satellite + real-time data → physics → Gemini → dispatch

1 · Data feeds

Google Earth Engine: Copernicus DEM, JRC water, WorldPop, IMERG per asset. NASA VIIRS/IMERG imagery. Open-Meteo real-time 72 h. IMD tracks.

→

2 · Hazard ensemble

40 Monte-Carlo tracks. Holland wind, shelf-amplified storm surge, rainfall ponding. Hemisphere-aware.

→

3 · Vulnerability

Fragility curves for 10 asset classes, power cascade and road-access cascade → P(disruption), P(cut off).

→

4 · Gemini 3.7 Flash

Satellite image + model JSON → situation, pathways, advisories in Odia / Telugu / Bengali.

→

5 · Dispatch

IMD colour-coded district advisories → CAP 1.2, webhook, e-mail, WhatsApp; auto-dispatch.

AI approach

Gemini 3.7 Flash as the duty analyst

Input: NASA VIIRS image, 2 May 2019, sent with the model output

  • Multimodal: reads the eye, cloud organisation and which coast is under the rain bands
  • Grounded: gets the model's KPIs, top-risk assets, pathways and advisories as JSON, and is told never to invent numbers
  • Cross-checks imagery against model hotspots and states where they disagree
  • Structured JSON output: situation, key risks, rainfall damage pathways with mitigations, confidence, caveats
  • Multilingual: SMS-ready advisories in English + Odia, Telugu, Bengali, attached to the dispatch
  • Reliable: fallback across Flash models, back-off, key kept server-side on Cloud Run

Automated early warning

From forecast to the right desk, automatically

RED ORANGE YELLOW district advisories on the IMD colour code

  • Routed to the District Collector & DDMA, the municipal body of each affected town, OSDMA / APSDMA / WB DM&CD, the DISCOM and the District Health Officer
  • CAP 1.2 XML: the format India's SACHET platform uses
  • Webhook to a Cloud Run relay → control-room inbox, Google Chat, state gateway; plus e-mail and WhatsApp
  • Auto-dispatch after every run, once per district and level

Who it serves

Built for the 72 hours before landfall

District Collectors & DDMAs

Who to evacuate, to which shelter, which shelters are themselves exposed or cut off.

Municipal bodies (ULBs)

Town-level advisories, pumps and tree-clearing on the arterial roads that will flood.

State SDMAs & EOCs

OSDMA, APSDMA, WB DM&CD: statewide RED/ORANGE picture, CAP feed for SACHET.

Power utilities (DISCOMs)

Substations and line spans likely to fail → stage crews and mobile transformers.

Health departments

Hospitals losing grid power or access → fuel, generators, 72 h stock, transfers.

Citizens, via authorities

Advisories in Odia, Telugu and Bengali, ready for SMS and WhatsApp.

Scale of benefit in the Fani replay alone: 12.3 lakh people in ≥ 118 km/h winds, 9.0 lakh likely without power, 4.6 lakh residents flagged for evacuation.

Why it's deployable

A state could pilot it within weeks

  • Zero install: a web app; works on any browser in a control room
  • Serverless on Google Cloud: one gcloud run deploy; scales to zero between storms, pay-per-use
  • Standards-based: CAP 1.2 output for SACHET; IMD colour codes; district and ULB routing
  • Key-safe: Gemini key stays server-side; the static site shows cached AI output
  • Open source (MIT) and tested

Pilot plan: one state, 4 weeks

Week 1: load state assets from OSM / Bhuvan / DISCOM GIS; run the GEE pipeline.

Week 2: calibrate fragility on the last 2–3 cyclones' damage reports.

Week 3: connect the dispatch webhook to the state EOC and SACHET test gateway.

Week 4: shadow mode alongside IMD bulletins; human sign-off before any Actual alert.

CycloneSentinel10

How it scales across India — and BRICS

New coast = new data, same engine

  • India: add Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Kerala by adding towns, tracks and assets. The engine, cascades, advisories and CAP are unchanged.
  • Languages: Gemini writes advisories in each state's language (Tamil, Gujarati, Marathi, Malayalam…)
  • BRICS: hemisphere-aware physics (tested) serves typhoons in China, the Mozambique Channel (South Africa), the Arabian Gulf (UAE, Iran) and Brazil
  • CAP 1.2 is the WMO/ITU standard used by national alert systems worldwide
